# Logo

The ZatcaWeb mark and wordmarks, drawn inline so they follow the theme.

**Props:** `variant` — `'full'` (default), `'arabic'` (RTL lockup, mark on the right) or `'mark'`; `size` — mark height in px (default 32).

- Use `'arabic'` in the Arabic sidebar and Arabic marketing, `'full'` in English, `'mark'` in collapsed nav, favicons and avatars.
- Minimum: mark 16px, wordmark 96px wide. Clear space = half the mark height.
- Colours come from `logo-tile`, `logo-glyph`, `accent-500`, `ink` and `brand-600`; never override them.
