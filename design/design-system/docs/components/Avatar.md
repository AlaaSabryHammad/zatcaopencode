# Avatar

Initials or photo for people (round) and organizations (square), with optional presence dot.

**Props:** `name` (initials and tint derived from it; Arabic names drop "شركة/مؤسسة" and "ال"), `src`, `size` (`xs`–`xl`), `square` (organizations), `status` (`online` · `away`). `AvatarGroup` takes `names`, `max`, `size`.
